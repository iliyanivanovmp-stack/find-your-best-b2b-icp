# ICP scoring rubric

Score every criterion from 1 to 10 using the anchors below. Scores between anchors require an evidence-based explanation. Every score cites one or more evidence IDs.

Weighted total:

`sum(score × weight)`

The result is on a 1 to 10 scale.

| Criterion | Weight | Score 1 | Score 5 | Score 10 |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Pain severity | 20% | Nice-to-have inconvenience with no measurable consequence | Recurring operational problem with visible cost or risk | Existential, regulated, revenue-critical, or repeatedly budgeted problem with quantified consequence |
| Trigger strength | 15% | No observable event or timing pressure | One plausible trigger that sometimes creates action | Multiple current, observable triggers reliably tied to budget and urgency |
| Ability to pay | 15% | No budget owner or economics cannot support the offer | Budget exists but competes with other priorities | Clear budget owner, established category spend, and affordable payback |
| Reachability | 15% | Buyers are unidentifiable or inaccessible through available channels | Buyers are identifiable but noisy, gated, or expensive to reach | Buyers and champions are identifiable with precise filters and reachable through proven channels |
| Solution and delivery fit | 15% | Requires capabilities, proof, integrations, or compliance the seller cannot provide | Core outcome fits but material customization or new proof is required | Existing delivery system, proof, integrations, and constraints match with minimal customization |
| Time to revenue | 10% | Typical path exceeds 12 months or depends on major procurement | Three to six months with manageable stakeholders | Buyer can approve a paid pilot or project inside 30 days |
| Deal potential | 10% | Deal is too small or one-off to justify acquisition and delivery | Acceptable first project with moderate expansion | Strong first contract, repeatability, expansion, and retention potential |

## Disqualifiers

An active disqualifier prevents a candidate from winning regardless of total score. Examples:

- required certification, regulated authorization, or data residency cannot be met
- minimum viable delivery exceeds current capacity
- acceptable payback cannot support the price floor
- buyer cannot be reached through any permitted channel
- sales cycle exceeds the operator's stated limit
- the problem is outside the product or service's actual mechanism

Keep potential risks separate from active disqualifiers. A risk lowers a criterion. A disqualifier removes eligibility.

## Tie break

Totals within 0.30 points are a tie. Break ties in this order:

1. Higher evidence confidence.
2. Higher time-to-revenue score.
3. Higher solution-and-delivery-fit score.
4. If still tied, report a tie and recommend the smallest falsification test. Do not invent precision.
